Getting ready

How to prepare

Pack

What to bring

ID or passport, comfortable clothes for Team Building, one smart-casual outfit for the Opening and Closing Ceremony, any personal medication, a reusable water bottle and a power adapter if needed.

Documents

Travel & consent

Minors need a signed parental consent form; international participants should check passport/visa requirements for Italy well in advance.

Weather

Verona in October

Expect mild, sometimes rainy autumn weather (roughly 10–18°C). Layers and a light waterproof jacket are recommended.

Your safety

Code of conduct & safeguarding

EYP operates under a Code of Conduct and Child Safeguarding Policy that apply to every participant, official and organiser. Every session has Event Safe Persons you can approach confidentially at any time. Their contact details are published in the Event's Hub once the session opens.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to speak fluent English?

No — good working English is enough. Sessions are a supportive environment to practise and improve.

What currency is used in Italy?

The Euro (€). Cards are widely accepted in Verona; a small amount of cash is useful for local shops and transport.

Are meals provided?

Yes. Menus and dietary/allergen information are published in the Event's Hub; please communicate dietary requirements through the official registration form.

Who do I contact if I have a problem during the session?

Any Event Safe Person, listed with contact details in the Event's Hub, or the session mailbox above.
