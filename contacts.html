Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to speak fluent English?

No — good working English is enough. Sessions are a supportive environment to practise and improve.

What currency is used in Italy?

The Euro (€). Cards are widely accepted in Verona; a small amount of cash is useful for local shops and transport.

Are meals provided?

Yes. Menus and dietary/allergen information are published in the Event's Hub; please communicate dietary requirements through the official registration form.

Is there a dress code?

Comfortable clothes for Team Building and Committee Work, and one smart-casual outfit for the Closing Ceremony.

Who do I contact if I have a problem during the session?

Any Event Safe Person, listed with contact details in the Event's Hub, or the session mailbox above.
