The host city

Why Verona?

A city shaped by history, craftsmanship and exchange — an ideal setting for a session about weaving local traditions into Europe's future. Verona has spent centuries at the crossroads of European culture, and it's that spirit of encounter we want participants to experience during RS Verona 2026.

Image placeholder

Essential places

Useful landmarks and meeting points.

Piazza Bra and the Arena, Ponte Pietra and the Adige riverside, Piazza delle Erbe and Castelvecchio are all within walking distance of the venue.

Image placeholder

Local traditions

Food, craft and stories from Verona.

Try risotto all'Amarone and local bakery pandoro, and look out for the city's centuries-old marble-working and printing traditions along the way.

Image placeholder

Good to know

Transport, safety and practical tips.

Buses run frequently from the station, Event Safe Persons are reachable throughout the session, and comfortable shoes are recommended for the historic centre's cobblestones.

Why we do this

Vision & goals

RS Verona 2026 follows the European Youth Parliament format: three phases designed to build trust, sharpen critical thinking, and turn debate into shared resolutions.

Team Building

Build trust

Delegates form their committees and learn to work as a team through non-formal activities, before a single policy debate begins.

Committee Work

Think critically

Committees research, debate and draft resolutions on real European challenges, guided by their chairpersons.

General Assembly

Decide together

All delegates debate and vote on the resolutions in a plenary session, practising public speaking and parliamentary procedure.
