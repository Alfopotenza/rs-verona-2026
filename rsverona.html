The host city

Why Verona?

For the first time in PEG's history, a Regional Session comes to Verona: a city shaped by history, craftsmanship and exchange, the ideal stage for weaving local traditions into Europe's future.

The session's motto, "Crafting Unity: Weaving Local Traditions into Europe's Future," speaks directly to Verona: an event that celebrates local heritage while staying true to the European outlook of every EYP session. We want participants to discover and share cultures and traditions, weaving places, countries and customs into a tapestry of memories and hopes for the future.

The session builds awareness of European citizenship, sparks debate on the questions facing European democracy, and strengthens participants' English communication skills. Locally, it's a chance to deepen ties with Verona and its institutions — a meeting point open to everyone, where local traditions are rediscovered through a European, pluralist lens.

Essential places

Where to go

Piazza Bra and the Arena, Ponte Pietra and the Adige riverside, Piazza delle Erbe and Castelvecchio are all within walking distance of the venue. Verona's historic centre is easy to explore on foot, through medieval lanes and views over the Adige.

Local traditions

What to try

Try risotto all'Amarone and local bakery pandoro, the sweet symbol of Verona's festive season. The city also keeps centuries-old craft traditions alive, from marble-working to printing, still woven into daily life today.

Good to know

Practical tips

Buses from Verona Porta Nuova station run frequently and connect quickly to the historic centre. Event Safe Persons are reachable throughout the session, and comfortable shoes are recommended for the historic centre's cobblestones.

Why we do this

Vision & goals

RS Verona 2026 pursues a threefold goal: to nurture active citizenship, to support personal growth, and to build a habit of dialogue and democratic debate.

Goal 01

Active citizenship

Through the activities of the European Youth Parliament we want young people to care about public life: to notice the challenges around us, to see how institutions work to address them, and to start thinking about problems actively.

Goal 02

Personal growth

Our simulations give hundreds of participants the chance to practise teamwork and public speaking, and to test themselves alongside new people outside the school setting. Staying in the network opens the door to new careers and to meeting older members already established in a wide range of fields.

Goal 03

Dialogue & democracy

Debate is at the heart of every session: learning to listen to views different from your own, to defend your ideas with arguments, and to work towards a shared position — hands-on training in openness to dialogue and democratic practice.
