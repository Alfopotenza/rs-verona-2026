Why stay involved

Reasons to continue

Four reasons to stay in the EYP network, even after the last day of RS Verona 2026.

Experiences

Live unique experiences across Europe

Joining the EYP network means having the chance to live unique experiences all across Europe, often for free, by getting involved as a volunteer and contributing directly to EYP's growth.

Mission

Contribute to a mission worth believing in

Being part of EYP means contributing to a mission that builds awareness among young people around European citizenship, multiculturalism, intercultural dialogue and active participation in society.

Growth

Grow and find your direction

EYP offers highly formative experiences that help you build skills, discover new interests and find your way toward future choices. It's also a chance to meet hundreds of capable, motivated young people working across many different fields and paths.

People

Build bonds that last

Beyond opportunities and skills, EYP is above all about people. It's a place to build genuine friendships and meaningful relationships, have fun, share experiences, and connect with people who share your passions and values.

What makes it all possible

EYP's officials

The backbone of the network is its officials: volunteers who, year after year and country after country, organise hundreds of events. Within events there are specific roles you can take on across the different teams — here's an overview!

Find out more →
  1. Head Organisers
  2. Core Organisers
  3. Organisers

Organising Team

The Organising Team builds the structure of the event. It handles fundraising (reaching out to companies, foundations and local institutions for partners who support the event), logistics, food and venues, and participant management. It's the team that works on the event the longest, and the one that makes it possible in the first place.

  1. President
  2. Vice Presidents
  3. Chairpersons

Academic Team

The Academic Team gives the event its substance. It coordinates every academic activity: choosing the topics to be debated, writing the topic overviews, running committee work and guiding the drafting of the resolutions.

  1. Editor / Editor-in-Chief
  2. Editorial Assistants
  3. Media Team Members

Media Team

The Media Team takes care of the event's communication and media output. It produces graphics, photos and video to tell the story of the event and leave every participant with a unique memory — and it manages the material shared with sponsors and the event's visual identity.

  1. Head of Jury
  2. Jury Members

Jury Team

The Jury Team follows and supports delegates' growth within the network. It's made up of experienced network members who observe committee work and offer feedback to help delegates improve — and who select delegates for other national and international events.

  1. Event Safe People

Safe Team

The Safe Team makes sure the network's policies are respected and looks after everyone's wellbeing. Activities within the EYP network are governed by a strict internal policy designed to protect and support all participants, regardless of background, ethnicity, religion or anything else.

Safe People are also a point of reference for any personal matter, for delegates and officials alike. Every event has a SAFE ROOM, where you can rest, relax, or simply step away from the pace of the event.

  1. NC (National Committee) Members

Other roles

Present in over 40 countries, the network is organised through National Committees. NC members (in Italy, holding the role of Board Advisors for the association) support every team to keep the event running smoothly and in line with the association's purpose and methods. They provide media support, help coordinate between teams, and represent the association during the event.

Questions

FAQ

Everything you were wondering about becoming an EYP official.

How can I become an official?

To become an official, you must register on the Members' Platform. After the session, you'll receive an email with all the information, and your NC will recognise your account (otherwise, for Italian delegates, send an email to hr@eypitaly.org asking for it).

In the Members' Platform, you can find all the events organised throughout the network, and you can apply to them for all the available positions!

What's the best way to start my official journey?

Firstly, we're so happy that you're thinking about it. We're REALLY looking forward to seeing you somewhere in Europe, in our sessions! The best way to start is to apply for junior positions, such as: organiser, media team member, and chairperson. After gaining some experience, you'll discover your interests and learn more about EYP's structure, so you'll be able to decide which team best fits you!

Is it hard to become an official?

No, it's not! Our network needs a lot of volunteers to stay alive and grow, so we're definitely looking for young, interesting, eager, and enthusiastic people like you!

Am I required to have special skills for each role?

Absolutely not! EYP isn't made up of experts or technicians; instead, it is built and carried on by young people like you, who are studying and developing their skills. No one will require you to be prepared in a specific field to join a role: we want to grow together and learn from each other!

What's an application, and how should I write one?

Applications are the means through which the team leaders of all the events select the officials who will take part in the event. Through your application, the team leaders are going to understand if you're motivated enough to take part in the event, if you're aware of your position's implications, etc.

We strongly encourage you to be yourself in your application: EYP is not a competition! You should be honest, talk about your abilities, and not be afraid to show some weaknesses. EYP is about growth: the important thing is to be eager to learn, discover new things, and experiment!

I'm not sure about my application: what should I do?

We suggest asking for an opinion from some experienced people! Maybe you can ask your chair.

Otherwise, the organising team has a special gift for you: you can send your application to the event's email address (rsverona2026@eypitaly.org), so one of our organising team members can provide you with their humble opinion about it! We don't promise to build your application from the ground up, but we'll be available to suggest how to improve its structure!

If I get rejected, what should I do?

In some cases, events receive a number of applications that exceeds the number of officials that the session can host. Because of this, the team leaders must reject some of the applications. If this happens to you, don't worry: it might happen! Don't think about it too much, and maybe politely ask for some advice on how to write better applications next time!

The second step is: look for another session and apply again! EYP is waiting for you!
